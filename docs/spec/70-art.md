# 70 - Art

Generated with fal FLUX Krea and FLUX Kontext dev through the HF router by `tools/art/gen.py` (idempotent, lints banned words, `--force` regenerates). Helpers: `sheet.py` (contact sheet), `tile.py` (2x2 tile check). Outputs under `public/art/`, listed in `public/art/index.json`.

## Style constant

Realistic painted concept art for a grounded near-future war game; overcast diffuse light; muted cold palette of slate blue-grey, wet concrete, dark steel, small accents of safety orange; matte, weathered.

Every prompt ends with: `Wordless and untitled, no lettering, no numbers, no logos, no text, full-bleed.`

Textures: flat top-down orthographic photographs, low-frequency blotches removed (blur subtraction), made seamless by an edge cross-fade (160 px) and resized back to 1024. Portraits generated 896x1152, saved 512x656; `hallen` and `aske` cropped 8% at the bottom/right to drop a stray signature. Backdrops saved 1344x768, sky 2048x1024.
