# The world past the board: sky, city dressing, raid surroundings and the title poster

Pass of 2026-11-22 (owner's ask: "make the game more pretty: title screen, building art, environments"). Everything here is **presentation only**: the rules never read it (as RULING MAP-9 for the raid props), no engine, AI or data file changed, and the pick meshes stay the only pointer targets (KB rule 25). Shots: `docs/art/shots/m8-*.png` (remade by `e2e/scenery.spec.ts`).

## 1. Shared pieces (`src/client/board/`)
| File | What it draws |
|---|---|
| `backdrop.tsx` | `SkyDome`: a sphere riding with the camera, a zenith-to-horizon gradient with soft poster bands at the horizon, a sun disc and halo, stars at night. `skyAt(hour)` gives the tone for a clock hour (day, dusk and dawn warm band, night); `eveningSky()` is the raid's fixed evening (RULING ART-1, sun from the same direction as `EveningLights`). `HorizonFog` keeps the scene fog on the horizon colour so the far city melts into the sky. `Skyline` instances rocket towers (shaft, coloured collar, stepped crown, needle) and slab towers, tinted per instance, after `mood-dusk-s62` |

## 2. The city (`src/client/city/`)
- `cityLayout.ts` (pure data, deterministic from `city.json`): a street on every second cell line (all district edges fall on them), so the play area is 36 blocks of 2 x 2 lots; every lot the rules leave empty gets dressing by district (Meridian Heights row houses, Sunbeam Row shops with awnings, Civic Centre plazas with fountains, The Works sheds and tanks, Parkside lawns, ponds and paths, Signal Hill houses with trees); an outer city four blocks deep on the south, east and west that stays low in front of the camera and climbs to the north-west behind the play area; a waterfront promenade and the bay to the north; the far towers across the bay.
- `CityDressing.tsx` draws it all instanced: asphalt, kerbs and pavement tinted by district, lane dashes and zebra crossings, the filler, trees, street lamps that glow after dark, the bay (a shader that reflects the sky and glints the sun), the monorail (a loop around the Civic Centre ring on pylons, a three-car train with lit windows at night) and civilian air traffic in enamel colours with head and tail lights at night.
- `CityScene.tsx`: the sky, sun (or moon) and fog follow the clock, the shown hour easing after the ten-minute ticks; shadow maps on (RULING ART-8); the 25 procedural buildings cast and take shadows, their plinths narrowed to the new pavement (0.76 of a cell), and their window strips cut into panes, a lead building's panes all lit and pulsing together (the hum, unchanged in meaning).
- The ambient clock (`ambient.t`) drives the train and the traffic and stops while the city is paused or a fight is open (RULING ART-7).
- `window.__ekcCity.setCamera(pose)` moves the city camera for shots.

### The strategic overlays (2026-11-22, PLAN D26)
The city grew to 24 x 24 (96 world units): the street grid, the filled lots, the outer city and the far towers follow `CITY.size`; the monorail loops the Civic Centre's own streets; the camera opens on the whole city (distance 135, up to 230) with fog and shadows sized to it. The filled lots inside the play area are kept to one storey, and the outer city is tinted cooler, so the 48 real buildings (floors now 2.1 units) stand above them. `CityOverlays.tsx` draws what the agency knows from its own view: each agent on a drive is a red-and-black car flying its leg (TRAVEL-1) with a dashed red route on the ground and a pulsing ring at its goal; a squad shares one larger van and parks at the door; a watcher's car parks at the lot; each building's last report is a ring in its band's colour (clean green, low mustard, some coral, heavy red) fading over four days; a building a slip named in the last day carries a pin card in the slip's tone. `labels.tsx` is a DOM layer beside the canvas (drei's Html made React unmount roots during render): the hovered and the selected building's name card (name, owner, last report, who is out there) and the district names when the camera is high. None of it takes a pointer (KB rule 25).

## 3. The title (`src/client/Poster.tsx`, `Title.tsx`)
A flat-colour poster above the console, all SVG: a teal-to-peach evening sky, a banded sun with a halo, drifting clouds, pale far towers, a teal middle city whose windows come on and off one by one, a monorail with its train crossing, civilian cars and the agency's red patrol car (black belly, two fan pods, after `patrol-car-b-s13`) on their lanes, and three cream rocket towers in front with mustard spines and teal and mustard collars. The console gains the lamp-test sweep on load (`presentation-concept.md` section 3). Motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Checked against `GAME-DESIGN.md` section 5: no names, no inspiration shapes; the saucer-on-a-column silhouette of the first draft was dropped (RULING ART-10).

## 4. Budget
The scenery switch (RULING ART-11) lives on the title and in the city console; `src/client/scenery.tsx` resolves it against the renderer's name. The city went from about 50 draws to about 90 (plus the shadow pass); every new piece is one instanced draw. The raid adds about 14 draws (sky, ground, pavement, dashes, three filler meshes, trees, four tower meshes) and no shadow maps, inside the e2e budget of 120 draws (`e2e/art.spec.ts`). Frame times on a real GPU were not measured in this pass (`node tools/perf.mjs` prints them).

## 5. Rulings
- RULING: ART-6 | The city and the raid stand inside a drawn world (sky dome, fog, outer city, bay, distant towers, raid street) | Presentation only, as MAP-9: the rules never read it and the pick meshes stay the only targets (KB rule 25); deterministic from the data, so screenshots repeat. provisional
- RULING: ART-7 | The train and the traffic run on an ambient clock that stops while the city is paused | Pause is the absence of ticks (the engine contract); a frozen city tells the player the clock is stopped. Clouds on the title and the water's ripple are not the city's life and keep moving. provisional
- RULING: ART-8 | Shadow maps in the city only; the raid keeps none | The city is a few dozen instanced draws seen from far; the raid keeps the integrated-GPU budget of `tile-set.md` section 4. provisional
- RULING: ART-9 | Dressing windows are cut by a shader from world position, lit by a stable per-pane hash (40% at night in the city, 45% in the raid's evening) | As ART-2: lit rooms carry no rules meaning, and a hash keeps saves and screenshots identical without per-window data. provisional
- RULING: ART-11 | A scenery switch: `auto` (the default) draws full on a GPU and plain on a software renderer (SwiftShader, llvmpipe) or before the canvas names its renderer; FULL and PLAIN can be picked on the title or beside LEAVE in the city, and are kept per browser under `ekc-view` | In software the dressing doubled a raid frame (125 to 213 ms in headless Chromium) and pushed the mouse-map e2e to its limit; a machine without a GPU should still play. Plain drops the raid's surroundings and the city's shadows and far towers. The e2e runs plain except `e2e/scenery.spec.ts`, which forces full and keeps the 120-draw budget. provisional
- RULING: ART-10 | The title poster has no saucer-on-a-column tower | The first draft's silhouette read as a real landmark (KB rule 29). provisional
