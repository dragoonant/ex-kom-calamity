## 3. Standing rules adopted
KB `rules.md` rules 1, 2, 6, 8-13, 19-30 apply without re-asking. Notes: rule 22 follows the real-time form (Q5); rule 25 applies because battles are on a tile grid; rule 6 limits each spec to the slice being built, so D2 is the target and the milestones below are the path. Rule 2 in force since M1: `docs/spec/10-rules.md` is the product, tests are named by its ids (`tests/coverage.test.ts` fails on an id without a test), and every ambiguity is a `RULING:` line there.

## 5. Out of scope for now
Multiplayer; a second city; anything from M5 on in the battle spec (KB rule 6: spec the slice being built). Not in M2 and waiting for their own slice: lifts, chain collapse, blast occlusion by walls, stray hits on units, glass that stops rounds. Not in M3: citizens that run (Q18), an agency autopilot in the client (the agency `normal` driver runs only in the bench), the infection of agents, sound as AI knowledge. Not in M4a: furniture as cover or as an obstacle (RULING MAP-9: props are dressing), textures, shadows, a time-of-day cycle (Q20), music beyond the five states of pass 2 (an announcer voice, adaptive layers). Not in M4b: animation of the figures (the rigged GLBs exist; the client does not play them yet, D8 note), tile textures, a re-UV of the generated meshes, the tier 1 agent and the citizen (no locked reference).
