From 3d-game-generation `templates/PLAN.md`. Rewrite, do not append. Owner decisions carry the owner's words and a date (KB rule 14); every open question has its default written here and work continues on it (KB rule 20). "ex-kom-calamity" is the repo and internal codename only (D10).

Before that: M4 done, 2026-11-18, and the second GPU figure pass (M4d) run 2026-11-20: M4a (the block's tile set and props, evening light and effects, the figure gallery, 23 synthesized sounds) and M4b (four generated figure models from the locked references on the owner's GPU, gated by measurement, `docs/art/figure-models.md`) merged and reconciled; M4d added the tier 1 agent and the two citizen bodies (D7, D22), so every figure role now draws a generated model: the tier 1 squad wears its own suit (RULING AGENT-1 closed, 2026-11-20), citizens and hidden hosts wear the two citizen bodies dealt by unit id (RULING CITIZEN-2), no stand-in is drawn unless a file fails to load. M4c (animation and effects, D21) is on its own branch. Playtest on GitHub Pages (D12). Audio pass 2, 2026-11-20 (D23): 36 generated sound takes behind the 23 synth names and five music states from ElevenLabs, measured and wired (`docs/audio.md`), not yet judged by ear. Next: the rest of the GPU pass (tile textures, the figures' redo list in `figure-models.md` 7), generated clips (D21 g), the audio redo list (`docs/audio.md` 4) once judged by ear, and the balance pass against the M3 baselines (Q9).
